On November 19, 2025, IonQ named Scott Millard as new Chief Business Officer (succeeding Rima Alameddine, who moved to an advisory role), and separately announced it was the only quantum company in Deloitte's 2025 Technology Fast 500 (citing ~2,000% revenue growth 2021-2024). The company also formed a quantum-networking research partnership with the University of Chicago this month. On its Q3 2025 earnings call (held in November), management disclosed ~$1B in submitted proposals, including potential large-scale government contracts. Shares closed November 28 at $49.30 (+5% on the day, +~18% for the week) amid active institutional buying, even as some analysts warned of a possible "quantum bubble."
