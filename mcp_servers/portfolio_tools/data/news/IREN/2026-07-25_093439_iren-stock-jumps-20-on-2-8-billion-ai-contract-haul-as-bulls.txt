IREN shares jumped as much as 20% after the AI-cloud firm raised its year-end annualized run-rate revenue (ARR) target to over $4B (from $3.7B), backed by $2.8B in new multi-year AI-developer cloud-service contracts (about 85% of the new ARR target already under contract). This follows an earlier two-part $5.5B Nvidia deal in May ($3.4B cloud services + up to $2.1B equity stake). IREN plans to deliver 480 MW of AI cloud capacity in 2026, rising to 1.2 GW in 2027, continuing its shift away from its Iris Energy Bitcoin-mining roots. Consensus price target raised to $29.55-77.20 range across sources, implying substantial upside from current levels.
