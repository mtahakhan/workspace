Deutsche Telekom shares have recovered from a 52-week low of €23.54 set in late June, closing recently at €27.01 (trading around €26.39 as of July 24). The company repurchased roughly 1.35 million shares between July 13-17 as part of its active buyback program, and is drawing attention for using AI to speed up Germany's fiber rollout and automate scheduling/site inspections. UBS and JPMorgan reaffirmed Buy ratings, though a price-target cut published July 21 reflects a more measured growth view. Next earnings: August 6, 2026.
