Intel shares surged 10% in early December after analyst Ming-Chi Kuo predicted Intel was nearing a deal to supply Apple starting Q2/Q3 2027 (shares gave back some gains the following Monday). On December 30, Intel rose 1.7% as Nvidia completed its $5B purchase of Intel shares. Intel finished 2025 up roughly 86%, capping a year of "stabilized resurgence" under new leadership, the US government's equity stake, and successful high-volume manufacturing of the 18A process node.
