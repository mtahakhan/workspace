Title: SAP Stock Rises As Record Cloud Backlog Fuels AI Momentum
Fetched At: 2026-07-25T09:34:39+02:00

Retrieved For: portfolio-daily-analysis (2026-07-25 run)

SAP published Q2/H1 2026 results on July 23: EPS EUR1.59 (vs EUR1.50 a year earlier), revenue EUR9.88B (vs EUR9.03B). Shares rose 7.18% initially and accelerated to +9.36% by July 24, driven by a record current cloud backlog of EUR22.9B (+27%) and cloud revenue growth of 22-24%, well ahead of large-cap software/European tech peers; management reaffirmed FY26 cloud revenue guidance of EUR25.8-26.2B with strong double-digit growth. This follows an internal memo on July 2 that froze nearly all non-AI recruitment, paused non-AI business travel and cut supplier spend - cost actions that had fueled margin/growth concerns and contributed to the stock being down ~32% YTD before this earnings-driven rebound.
