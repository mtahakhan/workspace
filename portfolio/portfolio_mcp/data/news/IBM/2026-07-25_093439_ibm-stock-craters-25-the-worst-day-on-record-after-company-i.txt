Title: IBM stock craters 25%, the worst day on record, after company issues second-quarter earnings warning
Fetched At: 2026-07-25T09:34:39+02:00
Fetch Method: WebSearch (query: "IBM stock news July 2026")
Retrieved For: portfolio-daily-analysis (2026-07-25 run)

IBM stock fell ~24-25% on July 14 after a surprise pre-earnings warning - its worst single day on record, surpassing the October 19, 1987 crash (-23.7%). CEO Arvind Krishna cited weakness in software/infrastructure as clients shifted quarterly capex toward hardware (servers, storage, memory) to secure supply-constrained inventory ahead of expected price increases. IBM guided Q2 sales to $17.2B (vs $17.85B estimate) and non-GAAP EPS to $2.93 (vs $3.02 estimate). A securities-fraud investigation into alleged pipeline manipulation has also surfaced. The formal Q2 report landed July 22-23, confirming the miss and a lowered FY26 outlook.
