Deutsche Bank upgraded Bayer to Buy and lifted its price target to €60 from €45 following a Supreme Court ruling on glyphosate liability; DZ Bank also moved to Buy with a €51 target, citing a clearer litigation framework. Goldman Sachs, however, removed Bayer from its European Conviction List. A fair-value reassessment lifted the central valuation mark from €34.97 to €54.92 per share, reflecting contained-but-ongoing litigation risk and portfolio execution. Bayer also announced a strategic alliance with the University of Colorado Anschutz, UCHealth and Children's Hospital Colorado on drug development. Next earnings: August 4, 2026.
