SAP SE posted Q2 2026 results on July 23 after market close. On July 24, 2026, SAP shares rose 7% in early trading after robust second-quarter results, particularly in cloud services, and continued climbing into July 25. SAP earned non-IFRS profit per basic share of EUR 1.59 on revenue of EUR 9.88 billion, versus analyst expectations of EUR 9.85 billion. The company raised full-year non-IFRS operating profit guidance from EUR 11.8-12.2 billion to EUR 11.9-12.3 billion. The standout metric was a record cloud backlog of EUR 22.9 billion, up 27%, showing strong demand for SAP cloud and ERP suite offerings.
