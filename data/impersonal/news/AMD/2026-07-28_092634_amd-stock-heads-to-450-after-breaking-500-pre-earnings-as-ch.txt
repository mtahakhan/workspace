AMD shares reversed below $500 on July 27, 2026 as chip stocks came under renewed selling pressure from AI-spending concerns, elevated valuations, Chinese competition and pre-earnings anxiety, after soaring 131% in the first half of 2026. This came days after AMD's Advancing AI event on July 22-23, which launched 2nm Zen 6 "Venice" server CPUs and updated the Helios rack-scale roadmap; Helios with the MI455X GPU carries 432GB of high-bandwidth memory versus 288GB on Nvidia's Vera Rubin.
