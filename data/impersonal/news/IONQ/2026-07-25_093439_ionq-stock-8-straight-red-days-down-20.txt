IonQ fell 7.1% on July 7 and moved lower for 8 consecutive sessions, erasing ~$4.1B of market value (-20.5% cumulative) to roughly $16B, amid investor caution over stretched quantum-computing valuations and increased competition following IQM Quantum Computers' Nasdaq listing. Despite the share-price weakness, IonQ reported its 'biggest quarter in company history' with $64.7M revenue (+755% YoY) and raised full-year guidance, though it faces projected operating losses of $310-330M for FY26. Sector commentary describes quantum names' 'extreme price-to-sales multiples' as highly speculative.
