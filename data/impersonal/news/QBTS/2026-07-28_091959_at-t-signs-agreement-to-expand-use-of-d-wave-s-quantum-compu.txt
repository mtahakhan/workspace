Title: AT&T Signs Agreement to Expand Use of D-Wave's Quantum Computing Technology Across Network Operations

Primary release (July 27, 2026): AT&T signed an agreement to expand use of D-Wave's annealing quantum computing technology across its network operations, initially layering it into tools already powering AT&T's agentic AI solutions, with a reported 240x network speedup. AT&T is also evaluating D-Wave's forthcoming gate-model systems for quantum security and quantum communications. Same day, D-Wave completed its voluntary listing transfer from NYSE to Nasdaq, with CEO Alan Baratz ringing the opening bell. QBTS closed at $19.51, up 20.4%, on volume ~54% above its three-month average, lifting IonQ and Rigetti in sympathy. Significance: a named commercial enterprise customer is the first real counter-evidence to the July quantum de-rating, which was driven by price-to-sales multiples rather than by any monetization failure.
