QBTS fell 29.4% month-to-date in July 2026, underperforming the Computer and Technology sector's -1.4% and the S&P 500's +1.2%, and is down 33.7% from its highs after a 211% surge in 2025. The decline is attributed not to business deterioration but to the broader quantum sector selling off as late-2025 enthusiasm cooled: profit-taking after outsized gains, valuation concerns across high-growth quantum names, and a macro backdrop of elevated Treasury yields and higher-for-longer Fed expectations. Additional pressure came from classical-simulation research in Science challenging D-Wave's published quantum-supremacy results, and a Form 144 filing signalling a potential supply overhang. Context: this is the same multi-week de-rating as IONQ, and it is the backdrop against which the July 27 AT&T expansion (+20.4% session, already stored) reads as the first real monetization counter-evidence.
