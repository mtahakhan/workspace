Cantourage Group SE operates as a medical cannabis company in Europe and internationally, offering cannabis flowers, extracts, dronabinol, THC and CBD products, listed in Frankfurt under ticker HIGH. Group EBITDA rose to EUR 5.7 million as the company navigated temporary pricing challenges in Germany through a focus on premium strains and increased sales in the UK and Poland; UK sales alone contributed over 20% of total group revenue in 2025. Cantourage forecasts sales exceeding EUR 100 million for FY26 despite ongoing market challenges. The shift toward premium offerings in Germany aims to improve profit margins, alongside a strategic transformation to a principal buyer model. Market cap is EUR 66.59M; next earnings report September 2, 2026.
