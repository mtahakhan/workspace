The global semiconductor selloff deepened on Tuesday July 28, 2026 as sentiment worsened over the sustainability of the AI boom. South Korea's Kospi fell as much as 8.2%, triggering a 20-minute exchange trading suspension, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix each down at least 9%. Japan's Nikkei 225 and Taiwan's Taiex fell more than 4%. This follows earlier July weakness: the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) posted its third weekly decline in four, dropping almost 9%, after TSMC raised its capex forecast alongside a better-than-expected Q2. Investors are questioning whether AI-driven valuations have run ahead of fundamentals.
