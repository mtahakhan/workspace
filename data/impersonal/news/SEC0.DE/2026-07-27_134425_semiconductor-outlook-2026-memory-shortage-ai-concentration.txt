The semiconductor industry is navigating a high-stakes paradox in 2026, with soaring AI-driven demand pushing revenues to unprecedented levels. However, the industry has largely placed its eggs in the AI basket, presenting concentration risk.

Memory shortage: severe shortages in essential components such as memory are projected to drive 50% price spikes by mid-year. AI-driven demand is creating the biggest memory shortage in history, with DRAM and NAND prices expected to remain elevated through the rest of the decade.

Structural shifts: semiconductor growth in 2026 is being reshaped by memory constraints, faster product cycles, new security requirements, and robotics. Chipmakers that improve memory efficiency and align with high-value AI and data center demand will be in a stronger position than those tied too closely to volume consumer markets.

Market growth: projected to exceed US$1 trillion in 2030, from the current US$627 billion, driven by exploding demand for AI chips and related products.
