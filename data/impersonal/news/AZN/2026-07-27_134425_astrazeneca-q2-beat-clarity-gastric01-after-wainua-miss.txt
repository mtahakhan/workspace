On July 9, 2026, AstraZeneca stock fell more than 8% premarket after the Phase III CARDIO-TTransform trial of Wainua missed its primary endpoint. The stock closed down 6%, its worst day in over two years.

Q2 2026 earnings beat: core EPS climbed to $2.63 versus consensus of $2.48, revenue approximately $15.4 billion. Growth was powered by the oncology franchise, particularly breast cancer drug Enhertu, and the rare disease portfolio. Shares rose 1.6% on the print.

Pipeline: on July 26, AstraZeneca announced that antibody-drug conjugate sonesitatug vedotin showed a statistically significant improvement in overall survival in the CLARITY-Gastric01 Phase III trial, restoring some pipeline confidence after the Ultomiris Phase III failure and the earlier Wainua miss.

Analyst view: average rating "Strong Buy" across 10 analysts, 12-month target $221.09 (~31% above the latest price). Full-year 2026 guidance maintained; CEO Pascal Soriot says the group remains on track for its $80 billion total revenue ambition.
