Title: AstraZeneca H1 Results: $30.7B Revenue, Core EPS Up 12%

AstraZeneca posted mixed second-quarter 2026 results, beating Wall Street's earnings estimates while falling short on revenue, with H1 revenue of $30.7 billion and core EPS up 12%. The company reported disappointing late-stage trial results for two experimental therapies, including a Phase 3 study of Ultomiris. In July AstraZeneca signed an exclusive license agreement with Dizal Pharmaceutical for Zegfrovy (sunvozertinib) with a $900m upfront payment upon achievement of development, regulatory and sales milestones, and an exclusive licence with Chia Tai Tianqing for PDE3/4 inhibitor TQC3721 for respiratory indications. CEO Pascal Soriot said the company is "on track to deliver our $80bn Total Revenue ambition, which assumes successes and setbacks".
