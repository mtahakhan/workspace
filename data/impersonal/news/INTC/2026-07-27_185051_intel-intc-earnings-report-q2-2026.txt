Intel's Q2 2026 results (July 23) showed 25% revenue growth, the fastest for any period in almost 15 years, with Data Center and AI revenue up 59%. Earlier in July, INTC fell 7.67% on July 8 to $110.68, extending a 21% weekly slide after Intel's 18A yield delay and AMD's first-ever data-center revenue lead. Intel shares are up over 170% in 2026 after soaring 84% last year, but the stock dropped 28% in July.
