Intel's Q2 2026 revenue grew 25%, its fastest for any period in almost 15 years, with Data Center and AI revenue up 59% - but the stock sold off sharply on rising capital spending and foundry losses, with investors questioning the sustainability of the AI investment. As of July 27, 2026 the stock ranged $86.94-$94.98, last $90.95. Earlier in the month Intel fell 7.67% on July 8 extending a 21% weekly slide after an 18A yield delay and AMD taking its first-ever data-center revenue lead, following a Bank of America note on July 1 that the AI chip space was becoming overvalued; it then rebounded 4.50% to $107.76 on July 15 on AI chip demand.
