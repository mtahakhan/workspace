Bayer has moved its US glyphosate/Roundup business into a separate legal subsidiary, fuelling break-up speculation and materially recasting the investment case by ring-fencing litigation risk. This sits alongside a Supreme Court ruling on glyphosate and a wave of analyst upgrades: the central valuation mark was lifted from EUR 34.97 to EUR 54.92, Deutsche Bank upgraded to Buy from Hold on July 2, Goldman Sachs maintained Buy on July 6. Coverage now frames the stock as having rallied 83% and asks whether it can stay cheap. A make-or-break August hearing and a Bundestag vote on glyphosate remain open items. Next earnings August 8, 2026. This is the structural cause of the +34.4% 56-day move - a re-rating on litigation-risk containment, not a momentum spike.
