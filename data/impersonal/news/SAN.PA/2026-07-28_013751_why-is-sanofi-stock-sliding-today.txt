Sanofi fell 1.7% to €75.04 after halting all clinical development of amlitelimab (atopic dermatitis), an asset from the $1.1B Kymab acquisition that had been seen as a potential Dupixent successor. Executive leadership reshuffle effective September 1. Full Q2 earnings due July 30. Analyst consensus remains Buy, ~31% upside to target.
