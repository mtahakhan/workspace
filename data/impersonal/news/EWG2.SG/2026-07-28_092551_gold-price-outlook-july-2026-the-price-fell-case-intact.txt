Gold traded near $4,038/oz on July 13, 2026, roughly 28% below its January all-time high of $5,595, and was little changed around $4,050/oz late in the month after a nearly 2% drop the prior session as investors watched Middle East developments for energy-driven inflation risk. Investors await the Federal Reserve meeting (July 29), where rates are widely expected to be held, with markets pricing roughly an 80% chance of a hike in September. JP Morgan forecasts $6,300/oz by end-2026; Deutsche Bank sees above $6,000, UBS $6,200, Société Générale $6,000.
