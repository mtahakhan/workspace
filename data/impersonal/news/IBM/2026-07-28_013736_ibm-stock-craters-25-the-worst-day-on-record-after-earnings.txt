IBM shares crashed 25% on July 14 (worst day on record, surpassing Oct 1987) after a surprise Q2 warning. CEO Arvind Krishna cited clients shifting capex toward servers/storage/memory ahead of expected price increases, hurting software/infrastructure. Q2 revenue came in at $17.2B (+~1% YoY, far below double-digit growth estimates); 2026 forecast lowered.
